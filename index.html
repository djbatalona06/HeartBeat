What the app does

Mood
Hunger, joy and moody, 1–10, both of you side by side.
Exercise
A workout log, and camera proof that you did it.
Work
A shared calendar, filled from a file you export.
The pet
Gains XP when either of you logs. Neglect it and it sulks.

Getting it on your phone

  1. Open the app link in Safari Not Chrome, and not the browser inside Instagram or Messages. iPhone only lets web apps install from Safari.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen Not cosmetic. iOS refuses to deliver notifications to a web app unless it was added to the Home Screen and opened from that icon.
  3. Open it from the icon, then allow notifications The prompt only appears on a tap inside the installed app. Decline by accident and the only way back is to delete the icon and re-add it.
  4. Tap the invite link One tap and the two phones are paired. No account, no password, no phone number.

Privacy

Your phone holds the record. The server keeps a copy so the other phone can read it — that is the whole reason it exists.

Mood, exercise, cycle and calendar entries are readable only by the two devices paired to your couple. Photos and camera proof stay on your own phone and are never uploaded. No accounts, no analytics, no third parties.

Invite links expire after fifteen minutes and work exactly once. A couple is two people; a third join is refused.

Building it yourself

npm install then npm run dev. The app is Vite, React and TypeScript on Cloudflare Pages; the Worker is Cloudflare D1. The README covers deployment and the design doc explains why each piece is shaped the way it is.
