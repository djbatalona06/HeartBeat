I built us an app. It's called HeartBeat — it keeps our moods, workouts, cycles and calendars in one place, and it nudges us when the other one needs something. Here's how to get it on your phone.

1
Open the link in Safari
Not Chrome, not the in-app browser inside Instagram — actual Safari. iPhone only lets web apps install from there.
2
Share → Add to Home Screen
This part isn't optional or cosmetic. iOS refuses to send notifications to a web app unless it's been added to the Home Screen and opened from there.
3
Open it from the icon, then allow notifications
iOS only shows the permission prompt on a tap inside the installed app. If you say no by accident you have to delete the icon and re-add it, so — say yes.
4
I'll send you an invite link
Tap it once and we're paired. That's the whole setup — no accounts, no passwords, no phone number.
